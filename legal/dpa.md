# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — DRAFT

> ⚠️ **NOT LEGAL ADVICE — template only. Have counsel review before use.**
> This draft follows GDPR/UK GDPR concepts. Adapt for other regimes (CCPA, etc.).

This DPA forms part of the agreement ("Principal Agreement") between
[VENDOR LEGAL ENTITY] ("Processor"/"Supplier") and the customer ("Controller")
for the Mnema software ("the Software").

## 0. Architecture note (read first)
Mnema is **self-hosted on the Controller's own infrastructure**. In normal
operation, the Supplier does **not** host, access, or process the personal data
the Controller loads into the Software. In that configuration the Supplier is a
software supplier rather than a processor of Controller Content. This DPA governs
the **limited** situations where the Supplier does process Controller personal
data — principally **support engagements** where the Controller grants access to
its environment or shares logs/data for troubleshooting.

## 1. Definitions
"Personal Data", "Processing", "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", and
"Supervisory Authority" have the meanings in the applicable data protection law
("Data Protection Law").

## 2. Roles
The Controller is the controller of personal data processed via the Software.
Where the Supplier processes personal data on the Controller's behalf (see §0),
the Supplier acts as processor.

## 3. Scope and instructions
The Supplier shall process Controller personal data only:
(a) to provide the contracted services (e.g. support, deployment assistance);
(b) on the Controller's documented instructions, including this DPA; and
(c) as required by law (with notice to the Controller unless prohibited).

## 5. Confidentiality
The Supplier ensures persons authorized to process personal data are bound by
confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Security
The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures
appropriate to the risk (Data Protection Law, Art. 32 GDPR equivalent). The
Software's security architecture is described in the Security Whitepaper; for
support access the Supplier will use least-privilege, time-limited access and
secure channels.

## 7. Sub-processors
The Supplier shall not engage sub-processors to process Controller personal data
without the Controller's prior authorization and an equivalent data-protection
flow-down. Current sub-processors (if any) for support tooling: [LIST OR "NONE"].

## 8. Data subject rights
Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Supplier shall assist the
Controller (by appropriate measures, insofar as possible) to respond to data
subject requests. Because data resides in the Controller's environment, the
Controller can typically fulfil such requests directly.

## 9. Personal data breach
The Supplier shall notify the Controller without undue delay (and in any event
within [48] hours) after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting
Controller personal data processed by the Supplier, and shall provide reasonable
assistance.

## 10. International transfers
Where Supplier support involves transferring personal data across borders, the
parties shall ensure a valid transfer mechanism (e.g. Standard Contractual
Clauses) is in place. [Specify.]

## 11. Audit
The Supplier shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate
compliance with this DPA and allow for audits [on reasonable notice, no more than
once per year, subject to confidentiality], or provide a third-party report where
available.

## 12. Deletion or return
On termination, the Supplier shall delete or return Controller personal data in
its possession obtained for support purposes, and delete existing copies unless
law requires retention.

## 13. Liability and precedence
Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Principal
Agreement. In case of conflict on data protection matters, this DPA prevails.
