## 1. Scope
This notice explains how [ORGANIZATION] handles personal data within Mnema, an
internal AI assistant that answers questions from our internal documents.

## 2. What data we process
- **Account data:** username, email, role assignments.
- **Authentication data:** hashed passwords, session/refresh token identifiers.
- **Usage data:** your conversations and messages with the assistant.
- **Document data:** internal documents uploaded by administrators, which may
  contain personal data.
- **Technical logs:** request metadata and timestamps for security and
  troubleshooting (request IDs, IP address, actions taken).
