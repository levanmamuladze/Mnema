# GitHub billing & our contract

Our organisation's GitHub subscription details. (Contains
**organisation-specific** figures — restricted to DevOps.)

## Our plan

- **Plan:** GitHub Enterprise Cloud.
- **Seats:** 120 licensed seats (currently 104 used).
- **Annual contract value:** **€86,400** (€60/seat/month, billed yearly).
- **Renewal date:** **March 1** each year. Notice of seat changes must be
  given 30 days before renewal.
- **Account manager:** billing handled through our reseller, Northwind
  Software Licensing.

## Add-ons we pay for

- **GitHub Advanced Security** — enabled org-wide (secret scanning, CodeQL,
  dependency review).
- **GitHub Copilot Business** — 60 seats.
- **Extra Actions minutes** — a top-up bundle of 50,000 minutes/month
  beyond the included quota; our self-hosted `northstar-01` runner absorbs
  the heaviest jobs to keep this bill down.

## Cost controls

- Unused seats are reclaimed at each quarterly access review.
- Heavy CI is routed to the self-hosted runner instead of GitHub-hosted
  minutes.
- Copilot seats are assigned on request and revoked when someone leaves a
  coding role.

## Who can see this

Billing figures are **DevOps-restricted**. Engineers and general staff
should not have access to contract values or seat economics — direct
billing questions to the DevOps lead.
