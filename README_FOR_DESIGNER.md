# Mnema — the quick rundown for you


## What it is, in one line

**Mnema is a smart assistant that answers a company's employees' questions using
only that company's own documents.**

Basically a coworker who has read every company handbook and can explain anything
in it, instantly, any time.

---

## Where the name comes from

**Mnema** comes from **Mnemosyne** — the Titan goddess of *memory* in Greek myth,
and the mother of all nine Muses. The idea being that memory is the source of all
knowledge. That's the whole concept of the product: it's a company's shared
memory. (Her full name is a mouthful to say, hence the shorter **Mnema** — but
that's the lore behind it.)

---

## The problem it solves

New employees ask the same questions constantly: *"How do I request time off?"*,
*"What's our deployment process?"*, *"Where's the brand guide?"* The answers
exist — but they're scattered across PDFs, wikis, and people's heads. Mnema pulls
all of that into one thing you can just **chat with**.

---

## Who uses it

- **Employees** — log in and ask questions, get answers from the documents their
  role is allowed to see.
- **Admins** — upload the company's documents, decide who can see what, and manage
  accounts.

---

## The core ideas behind it

1. **It only answers from real company documents.** It doesn't make stuff up — if
   the answer isn't in the docs, it says so instead of guessing.
2. **Everyone sees only what their role allows.** Two people in different roles can
   ask the same question and get different answers, because they're allowed to see
   different documents. No one stumbles into files they shouldn't.
3. **The admin builds the roles.** Nothing is fixed — the admin creates each role
   themselves (a sales role, a legal role, whatever the company needs) and gives
   the AI a little personality and focus for it. There's just one built-in
   "general" assistant as a fallback when no specific role fits.

---

## What an employee does

1. **Signs in.**
2. **Chats** — types a question like texting; the answer streams in word by word
   (there's a little "typing" animation while it thinks), and comes back nicely
   formatted — headings, bullet points, code, etc.
3. **Manages their chats** — past conversations live in a sidebar; they can
   reopen, rename, or delete any of them, or start a fresh one anytime.
4. **Signs out.**

If a question falls outside what their role is allowed to see, the assistant
politely declines instead of answering — and if something just isn't in the
company's docs, it says so rather than making it up.

## What an admin does

1. **Creates & edits roles** — makes up whatever roles the company needs (sales,
   legal, whatever) and gives each one a name and a focus area that becomes the
   AI's "personality" for that role.
2. **Uploads documents** — one file at a time, or a whole folder at once (with a
   progress bar as it imports). Handles PDFs, Word docs, text, and more.
3. **Tags them by role** — picks which roles can see each document, and can
   re-tag or delete documents later.
4. **Manages people** — creates accounts, assigns roles, removes users.

> Heads up: there are two levels of admin. A **full admin** can do everything
> above. A **role-admin** only manages their own area — they just get the
> Documents part, for the roles they own.

## The screens that exist

| Screen | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **Sign in** | The login screen. |
| **Chat** | The main thing — a sidebar of past conversations (open / rename / delete / start new) next to the conversation itself: the question box, streaming answers, the typing animation, and an empty "ask me something" state. |
| **Admin console** | Three areas: **Documents** (upload single files or whole folders, tag by role, re-tag, delete), **Users** (create, assign roles, delete), and **Roles** (create/edit each role's name, focus, and AI personality). |
